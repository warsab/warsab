## 👋 About

I design, build, ship and consult on production grade AI systems for business clients across the UK, USA and South Africa. That covers LLM powered applications, agentic workflows, RAG pipelines and intelligent automation.

Before AI, I spent 10+ years in business operations. That background shapes how I work. I start from the business problem and the process around it rather than the model, which is why the systems I build tend to survive contact with real users.

I architect across all the mainstream LLMs including Anthropic Claude, OpenAI, Google Gemini and Microsoft Copilot, with a focus on agentic system design, multi agent orchestration, retrieval augmented generation and applied machine learning. Most of my work starts with a problem a client is stuck on. From there I solve it whichever way it needs solving, whether that is an AI automation dropped into their existing stack, a standalone web application, or a bespoke solution built from the ground up.

I work as an AI Consultant and Developer at **Dixon AI**, an official **Anthropic partner**, and I was the primary driving force behind that partnership.

Away from the technical side I am humble, social and a genuine networker. I enjoy meeting people, swapping ideas, speaking at community events and learning from whoever is in the room.

> My independent consulting and product work runs under my own banner, **AI Phoenix**.

**My journey:** Operations & Data Analytics → Data Scientist → AI Engineer → AI Architecture & Development
